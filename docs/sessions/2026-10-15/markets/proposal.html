Predicted LTGP

Two ways to forecast 365-day gross profit

Actuals stay actuals. A forecast lives in its own column, is drawn dashed with a band, says "forecast", carries its method and run date, and never enters a verdict or a total.

Option A: maturity multiplier

Learns from older cohorts as a whole. "Past cohorts grew 4.5x in repeat GP between day 60 and day 365."

  • First-order GP stays actual; only repeat GP is projected: repeat GP at day N × median ratio (day 365 / day N) of the last 6 to 12 mature cohorts of the same market.
  • November and December cohorts use their own same-month-last-year ratio.
  • Small markets use quarterly cohorts. No forecast before day 30 or for markets under about 50 new customers a month.
  • Pure SQL, cheap, explainable. Needs 18+ months of history in the market.

Option B: BG/NBD + Gamma-Gamma

Learns from every customer: how often they buy, how likely they have silently left, how much GP an order carries.

  • Python job (PyMC-Marketing) on the VPS or Cloud Run, writes back to BigQuery. BigQuery ML has no such model.
  • Uses young customers too, so it can help small or young markets.
  • Breaks on seasonality, replenishment rhythm and subscriptions; its interval covers parameter noise only.
  • Needs about 1,000 customers and 150 repeaters in the fitting window.

Choose by backtest

Predict 365-day GP from day 30, 60, 90 and 180 for cohorts Jan 2024 to Sep 2025, using only data available at each freeze date. Compare MAPE, bias and band coverage against a naive baseline, per market. Switch to B only on a clear win (for example 3+ pp lower MAPE). Default when there is no time: A, repeat-only, median, non-Q4, quarterly cohorts for small markets, no forecast for tiny ones (Venev).
